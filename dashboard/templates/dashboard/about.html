Who We Are

A technology-driven approach to everyday transportation

NandiRide is designed around one simple idea: every journey should be convenient, transparent and dependable.

The platform brings together passengers and drivers while giving administrators the tools required to manage rides, users, vehicles, payments, locations and support operations from one central system.

Our technology-focused approach allows NandiRide to support different vehicle categories, digital ride management, driver assignment, live tracking, fare calculation and secure payment workflows.
